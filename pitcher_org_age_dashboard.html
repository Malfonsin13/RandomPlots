Conclusions

Volume king: COL leads the whole 2023–2025 pool with 3931.3 IP across 41 pitcher-seasons. STL is next at 3430.7.

Best efficiency among substantial samples: KC is best at 15.86 pitches/IP, with TB basically tied at 15.88. That means they got similar innings with less pitch burn than most clubs.

Do not over-read age 18. There are only 20 pitcher-seasons there, which is just 2.3% of the whole sample. Age 22 is the real bulk of the dataset at 369 pitcher-seasons (42.9%).

MIL is the clearest young-age standout. They lead age 18 in both volume and efficiency (407.0 IP, 15.65 pitches/IP) and age 19 is strong too (649.7 IP, 15.59).

MIA owns age 20 volume (859.3 IP), but TEX is cleaner on efficiency at age 20 among decent samples (15.70 pitches/IP). So Miami looks like load, Texas looks like load with less waste.

At age 21, HOU has the most total IP (1314.7) but not efficient (17.60 pitches/IP). STL is more interesting there: less volume, but elite efficiency and a high mean workload (105.1 mean IP, 15.63 pitches/IP).

No obvious hard IP caps jump off the page once you aggregate seasons. What shows up more often is wide org-age range behavior, which looks more like role mix or developmental spread than a strict organizational stop-sign.
